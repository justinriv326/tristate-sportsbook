How the lines work

Every starter gets an expected score from Sleeper's half-PPR projection. During games, a player's line is the points he already has plus his projection scaled by how much of his NFL game is left. Team totals add those up, and the win probability comes from how far apart the totals are and how much of the game is still left to play.

Moneylines carry a small house edge like a real book. Spreads and totals sit at -110 and the line itself moves. Everything recalculates from live Sleeper scoring every 30 seconds. It's for bragging rights only; no real money moves through this page.
